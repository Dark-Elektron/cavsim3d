# Ports and port modes

Ports decide what the S- and Z-parameters mean. These lessons go beyond one TE10 mode per
port.

1. **[Carry several modes per port](multimode_ports.ipynb)**: three modes on each port of a
   rectangular waveguide, the 6 × 6 S-matrix they produce, each mode's cutoff and
   transmission against the exact solution, and different mode counts per port.
2. **[Coaxial line with a dielectric window](../tem_transmission_line.ipynb)**: TEM ports on a
   stepped coaxial line with a ceramic window, compared with CST Studio Suite.

For microstrip (quasi-TEM) ports see [How to model a microstrip line](../../how-to/microstrip.md).

Background: [Ports and port modes](../../explanation/ports.md).
